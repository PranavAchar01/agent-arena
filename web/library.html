Replay LIBRARYsource on GitHub

Robot skills, learned from people on the open web.

Every skill below started as one sentence. An agent scraped openly licensed videos of real people, MediaPipe Pose segmented every one, and a simulated SO-101 copied the best of them joint for joint, checked in MuJoCo physics. Every skill is free to download and reuse, with every source credited.

Open source: skills are joint trajectories (50 Hz, SO-101 joint names) plus the human pose they came from; reuse them freely and credit the original authors listed with each skill. Simulated SO-101 in MuJoCo (Google DeepMind); pose by MediaPipe (Google); scraping by Scrapling inside throwaway sandboxes on Vultr and Docker. Videos are Creative Commons or public domain.
